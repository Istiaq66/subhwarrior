This policy covers the Subh Warrior mobile app (the "App"), operated by IS TECH ("we"). Subh Warrior is a 28-day Fajr and focused-work challenge app. It shows Fajr and other prayer times, sends reminders, tracks your streak, and ranks participants on a leaderboard.

Email addressOnly you, used to sign in

We do not show ads, sell your data or use it for advertising. You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Delete account in the App, or request deletion by email.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Username. You choose it. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard, so do not use your real name if you want to stay anonymous.
  • City. You pick a city, or let the App detect the nearest one. The city name is shown publicly on the leaderboard and is used for the "local" ranking.
  • Challenge entries. Your daily log: whether you prayed Fajr on time, whether you prayed at the masjid, minutes worked, a work description, work type and an optional reflection, plus your start date, current week and streak.
  • Sign-in details, only if you create an account. You can sign in with Google, or register with an email address and password. We use your email address (and, for Google, your name and profile identifier) to sign you in and keep your progress linked to you across devices. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. We do not display your email to other users.

If you do not create an account, the App signs you in anonymously. An anonymous identifier is created for you, with no name or email.

Location

The App asks for location permission only when you choose to detect your city. It uses your device location to:

  • find the nearest city, and
  • calculate prayer times (the calculation happens on your device).

Your exact coordinates are stored on your device and in a private record in our cloud database that only your own account can read. Other users never see them. Other users can see only your city name.

You can decline location permission and pick a city from the list instead.

Information collected automatically

Through Firebase services, the App collects:

  • Usage events, such as starting a challenge, logging a day, reaching a streak milestone, sharing your progress card, and opening a notification, plus screen views and app start-up timing.
  • Crash and performance data, such as crash reports, app version, device model, operating system version and performance timings.
  • Device and app identifiers, such as an app instance identifier and, because Firebase Analytics is included, the Android advertising ID. We use these for measurement and diagnostics only. We do not show ads and we do not use your data for advertising.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, which Firebase uses for aggregate analytics.

What we do not collect

We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. We do not collect payment information.

What other users can see

On the leaderboard, any user of the App can see your username, city name, current streak and total qualifying days. They cannot see your coordinates, your start date, your daily entries, your work descriptions, your reflections or your email address.

How we use information

  • To provide the App's features: prayer times, reminders, streaks, leaderboard and progress sharing.
  • To save your progress and restore it if you reinstall the App or change phones.
  • To fix crashes, measure performance and understand which features are used, so we can improve the App.
  • To meet legal obligations and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Notifications and the Fajr call

Reminders and the optional Fajr call are scheduled on your device. The Fajr call uses a full-screen alert so it can appear over the lock screen like an incoming call. It does not use your microphone or camera, and no call is placed or recorded. You can switch each reminder on or off in Settings.

Sharing your progress

When you tap Share, the App creates an image of your streak card on your device and hands it to the app you choose. We do not receive that image.

Service providers

We use Google services to run the App. They process data on our behalf and under their own policies:

  • Firebase Authentication (privacy policy)
  • Cloud Firestore, which stores your progress and the leaderboard (privacy policy)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (privacy policy)
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring (privacy policy)
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play Services (privacy policy)

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights, safety or property, or to investigate fraud or abuse.

International transfers

Google processes data on servers in several countries, which may differ from where you live. Google relies on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where the law requires them.

Retention and deletion

  • Your progress, username and city are kept while your account exists.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept for the periods set in the Firebase project and then deleted by Google.
  • Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

To delete your account and data, open the App and go to Settings > Delete account. This permanently erases, immediately:

  • your username reservation and leaderboard entry,
  • your private record (exact location, start date, daily logs),
  • your sign-in account (anonymous, Google or email), and
  • the challenge data, reminders and Fajr alarm stored on your device.

If you sign in with Google or a password, the App asks you to confirm it first. Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling the App alone does not delete data already stored in our cloud database, so use Delete account first.

If you can no longer open the App, email ahmedboby66@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or, if you used the App anonymously, send your username) and we will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. See how to delete your account.

Usage and crash data collected through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics is kept for the retention period configured in our Firebase project and is then deleted by Google. It contains no username or email, and it is not removed by Delete account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Email us to exercise these rights.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you may request to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Location permission: you can withdraw it at any time in your device's settings.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 16, or any higher age required by local law, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to your private record is restricted to your own account by database security rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you as required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it. Earlier versions are available on request.
