Delete your Subh Warrior account

You can delete your account and everything stored with it, from the app or by email. Subh Warrior is developed by IS TECH.

Delete it in the app

The fastest way. Deletion happens straight away.

  1. Open Subh Warrior and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to About and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete permanently. If you use a password, enter it first. If you use Google, choose your Google account when asked.

This works for every account type: Google, email and password, and guest accounts.

Request it by email

Use this if you can no longer open the app, for example after uninstalling it or losing your phone.

  • Send it from the email address linked to your account.
  • If you only used the app as a guest, include your username.
  • We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

Or write to ahmedboby66@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Subh Warrior account".

What is deleted

  • Your username and its reservation.
  • Your leaderboard entry: username, city, streak and total qualifying days.
  • Your private record: exact location, challenge start date, current week and all daily logs, including work descriptions and reflections.
  • Your sign-in account, whether Google, email and password, or guest.
  • When you delete in the app, also the challenge data, reminders and Fajr alarm stored on your phone.

What is kept

  • Usage and crash data collected through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. It contains no username or email. It is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase project, then deleted by Google.
  • Anything we are legally required to keep, if the law ever requires it.

Read the full privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your data. Data already stored in our cloud database stays until you delete your account, so delete it first, then uninstall.
