Subh Warrior

Wake for Fajr, pray on time, and use the quiet hours after it for focused work. Subh Warrior is an Android app that turns this into a habit over a 28-day challenge.

What the app does

Prayer times for your city

Fajr, sunrise and the day's prayer times, calculated on your phone for the city you choose, with several calculation methods.

Wake up for Fajr

A reminder before Fajr, or an optional full-screen call at Fajr that is harder to sleep through.

Log your morning

Each morning, record whether you prayed Fajr on time and how long you worked. A day qualifies when you do both.

Track your progress

See your streak, weekly progress and 28-day challenge, compare on a leaderboard, and share your streak.

How Subh Warrior uses your Google account

You can sign in to Subh Warrior with Google. The app uses your Google account only to sign you in and keep your progress linked to you across devices. It receives your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID. It does not post anything, read your contacts or access any other Google data.

Your email is never shown to other users. Only your chosen username, city name and streak appear on the leaderboard. Read the privacy policy for details, and the terms of service for the rules of use.
